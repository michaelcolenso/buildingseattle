Brand assets served at /brand/* by Workers static assets (see `[assets]` in wrangler.toml).

- `tokens.css` — design tokens from the buildingseattle design system (colours, spacing, radii, type) and the IBM Plex @font-face rules. Linked in every page <head> before the inline styles.
- `fonts/` — IBM Plex Sans, Sans Condensed and Mono (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
- `favicon.svg` — plan-view Parcel mark; switches to white/sky lots under prefers-color-scheme: dark.
- `apple-touch-icon.png` — 180 px app tile.
